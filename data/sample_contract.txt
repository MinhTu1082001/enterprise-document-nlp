MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2024 by and between Northwind Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Provider"), and Contoso Retail Holdings LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Client").

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where control means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of such entity.

1.2 "Confidential Information" means all non-public business, technical, financial or customer information disclosed by one party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with this Agreement, whether disclosed orally, in writing or by inspection, that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

1.3 "Deliverables" means all reports, software, documentation and other work product that Provider delivers to Client in the course of performing the Services, as described in a Statement of Work.

1.4 "Fees" means the amounts payable by Client to Provider for the Services as set forth in the applicable Statement of Work.

1.5 "Services" means the data analytics and consulting services described in one or more Statements of Work executed by the parties under this Agreement.

1.6 "Statement of Work" means a written document signed by both parties that describes the Services, the Deliverables, the Fees and the schedule for a particular project.

1.7 "Term" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

2. SERVICES

2.1 Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner in accordance with the applicable Statement of Work and the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 Client may request changes to the scope of the Services by written notice to Provider. Provider shall respond to any change request within ten (10) business days with a written estimate of the impact on the Fees and the schedule. No change shall be binding unless set forth in a written amendment to the Statement of Work signed by both parties.

2.3 Provider shall not subcontract any portion of the Services without the prior written consent of Client. Provider shall remain responsible for the acts and omissions of any approved subcontractor as if they were the acts and omissions of Provider.

2.4 Client shall provide Provider with timely access to the data, personnel and facilities reasonably required for Provider to perform the Services.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Client shall pay the Fees within thirty (30) days after receipt of a valid invoice from Provider. All Fees are stated in United States dollars and are exclusive of applicable taxes.

3.2 Any amount not paid when due shall accrue interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower, from the due date until paid in full.

3.3 If Client disputes any invoice in good faith, Client shall notify Provider in writing within fifteen (15) days after receipt of the invoice, and the payment period under Section 3.1 shall be suspended with respect to the disputed portion until the dispute is resolved. Client shall pay the undisputed portion in accordance with Section 3.1.

3.4 Provider shall keep accurate records of the time and expenses related to the Services and shall make such records available to Client upon reasonable request during the Term and for two (2) years thereafter.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 The Receiving Party shall hold the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in strict confidence, shall use such Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing or receiving the Services, and shall not disclose such Confidential Information to any third party other than its employees and contractors who have a need to know and who are bound by written obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Section 4.

4.2 Notwithstanding Section 4.1, the obligations of the Receiving Party shall not apply to information that (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party, (b) was rightfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure by the Disclosing Party, (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information, or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality.

4.3 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation or court order to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party may make such disclosure provided that it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice, to the extent legally permitted, and reasonably cooperates with the Disclosing Party in seeking a protective order.

4.4 Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party within thirty (30) days and shall certify such return or destruction in writing upon request.

4.5 The obligations in this Section 4 shall continue for a period of five (5) years after the termination or expiration of this Agreement, except that obligations with respect to trade secrets shall continue for so long as such information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Subject to payment of the applicable Fees, Provider assigns to Client all right, title and interest in and to the Deliverables. Provider shall execute such documents as Client may reasonably request to perfect such assignment.

5.2 Provider retains all right, title and interest in and to any tools, methodologies, templates and know-how owned by Provider before the Effective Date or developed independently of the Services (the "Provider Materials"). To the extent any Provider Materials are incorporated into the Deliverables, Provider grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use such Provider Materials solely as part of the Deliverables.

6. WARRANTIES

6.1 Provider warrants that the Services will be performed in accordance with generally accepted industry standards and that the Deliverables will conform in all material respects to the specifications in the applicable Statement of Work for a period of ninety (90) days after delivery.

6.2 Client's sole remedy for breach of the warranty in Section 6.1 shall be, at Provider's option, re-performance of the non-conforming Services or a refund of the Fees paid for the non-conforming Services, provided that Client notifies Provider of the non-conformity in writing within the warranty period.

6.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS SECTION 6, PROVIDER DISCLAIMS ALL WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 The aggregate liability of either party arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the total Fees paid or payable by Client under the applicable Statement of Work during the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

7.2 Section 7.1 shall not apply to a party's breach of Section 4, to a party's indemnification obligations under Section 8, or to damages arising from a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

7.3 In no event shall either party be liable for any indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, including lost profits, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Client and its Affiliates from and against any third-party claim alleging that the Deliverables infringe any patent, copyright or trade secret of such third party, and shall pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

8.2 Client shall notify Provider in writing within ten (10) days after becoming aware of any claim subject to Section 8.1, shall give Provider sole control of the defense and settlement of the claim, and shall provide reasonable assistance at Provider's expense.

8.3 Provider shall have no obligation under Section 8.1 to the extent a claim arises from modifications to the Deliverables made by Client or from the combination of the Deliverables with materials not provided by Provider.

9. TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of three (3) years, and thereafter renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current period (the "Term").

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to the other party, provided that Client shall pay for all Services performed through the effective date of termination.

9.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receipt of written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail.

9.4 Sections 4, 5, 7, 8 and 10 shall survive any termination or expiration of this Agreement.

10. GENERAL

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

10.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that either party may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all of its assets.

10.3 All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered by hand, by courier or by email with confirmation of receipt to the addresses set forth in the applicable Statement of Work.

10.4 This Agreement, together with all Statements of Work, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.
